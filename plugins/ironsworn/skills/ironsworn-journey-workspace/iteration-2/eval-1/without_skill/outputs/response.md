The people of Stillwater don't speak Deepcrag's name at night. You've noticed that. Over the past two days, while you gathered what provisions the settlement could spare, every elder and every weathered trapper angled around it — "the old fortress," "the northern ruin," "the thing in the marshes." You had to use the name yourself, and when you did, the room got quieter.

Three days north. The Blackfen — that's what the marsh is called, though the locals prefer not to use that name either — stretches from Stillwater's northern fence-posts to the base of Deepcrag's crumbled outer wall. Cold standing water, sink-mud that looks solid until it isn't, mists that move against the wind. The haunting isn't rumor. Two trappers went in six weeks ago. One came back. He doesn't speak now.

None of that changes what you have to do.

The settlement gate stands open behind you. Grey morning. The last of the fires are smoking down to ash in the longhouses. Somewhere overhead a crow traces a wide circle, going nowhere.

The road ends forty paces ahead, where the reeds begin.

## Determining the Journey's Rank

Three days of travel across actively dangerous, haunted terrain — the Blackfen is not simply remote, it is hostile in ways that go beyond weather and bog. Enemies, history, and whatever still moves in that dark water. This is a **Formidable** journey.

Three days is not that long in calendar terms, but through the Blackfen every hour is earned. Formidable rank means each Undertake a Journey hit marks **one box (4 ticks)** of progress — the journey presses hard, and the track fills slowly.

## Your Move: Undertake a Journey

When you're ready to step off the firm ground into the reeds, you'll **Undertake a Journey** and roll +Wits.

Roll **1d6 + Wits** as your action score, and **2d10** as your challenge dice.

- **Strong hit (action score beats both challenge dice):** You reach a waypoint and mark progress. Choose — **steady pace** (mark progress, no cost) or **push hard** (mark progress, +1 momentum, but suffer −1 supply).
- **Weak hit (action score beats one challenge die):** You reach a waypoint and mark progress, but the marsh claims something — suffer −1 supply.
- **Miss (action score beats neither challenge die):** You're waylaid before you've gone a mile. Pay the Price.
