# GM Response — Journey Start

---

The name Deepcrag sits heavy in the air the moment you speak it aloud. The folk of Stillwater don't say it lightly — three days north, across the Greymire, through ground that swallows sound and light alike. Haunted, they call it. Not the poetic kind of haunted. The kind where things that should be dead still move.

You know what you're walking into. That knowledge, at least, is honest weight to carry.

**Determining Journey Rank:**
Three days of travel across genuinely dangerous, ill-omened terrain — far enough to matter, hazardous enough to hurt. This is a **Dangerous** journey.

## The Departure

Dawn comes grey and reluctant to Stillwater. The settlement's edge is marked by a line of cairns — river stones stacked by hands long dead — and beyond them the marsh begins almost immediately, the firm ground dissolving into hummock and black water.

You check your pack. The provisions feel adequate for now. Your sword is loose in its scabbard. Somewhere behind you, a child watches from a doorway and doesn't wave.

The path north is barely a path — a smear of flattened reeds where others have tried before. Whether those others came back is a question no one in Stillwater answered when you asked.

The air smells of wet peat and something older beneath it, something you don't have a name for.

## Your Move

When you're ready to push into the marsh, you'll **Undertake a Journey** (roll +Wits). Zura's Wits is **1**, so roll 2d10 (challenge dice) and 1d6 (action die), then add 1.

- **Strong hit (action + 1 beats both challenge dice):** Mark progress (2 ticks), and choose — move at a **steady pace** (no cost) or **push hard** (mark an additional tick, but Zura suffers -1 supply).
- **Weak hit (beats one challenge die):** Mark progress (2 ticks), but the marsh takes its toll — lose **1 supply**.
- **Miss (beats neither):** Pay the Price. No progress marked.
