# GM Response: Beginning the Journey to Deepcrag

The marshland north of Stillwater has a name among the locals — they call it the Greyfen, and they say it only in whispers. Three days of cold water around your boots, of mist that never quite burns off, of sounds that don't belong to any living thing you've named. Deepcrag sits at the northern edge of it, a broken tooth against the sky, and whatever waits inside has been waiting a long time.

You've resupplied. You've said what goodbyes felt necessary. Your hound is restless at your side, nose working the air that drifts down from the north. There's iron in that wind.

**Before you step off the road, we need to track this journey formally.**

*The journey track is created: Troublesome rank. Three segments to fill.*

## Undertake a Journey

Now you roll. To **Undertake a Journey** you roll +Wits.

**Roll +Wits now.**

- **Strong hit:** You make good ground. Mark progress. Choose to push on or make camp.
- **Weak hit:** You mark progress but face a peril — exhausting terrain, a lurking threat, ground that shifts underfoot.
- **Miss:** Trouble finds you before you've gone a mile. Pay the Price.

The hound presses its flank against your leg. The road ends here. What do you roll?
