## Waypoint — The Ashwood Ford

The grey morning gives way to a slow drizzle as Zura picks her way down a 
switchbacking trail toward the sound of running water. The tree line breaks, 
and she finds herself at a wide, boulder-strewn ford where a dark river — 
swollen with recent rain — cuts across the road to Deepcrag. The stones are 
slick and the current pulls hard at her boots as she crosses, soaking through 
to the bone. On the far bank she finds the charred shell of a woodcutter's 
waystation, long since burned out, its beams half-collapsed into a tangle of 
wet ash.

She rests here only long enough to wring out her cloak and check her pack. 
The supplies have not been generous; the crossing wasted time and wet what 
she carried.
