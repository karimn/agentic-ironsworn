## Narrative

The trail toward Deepcrag winds through a shallow ravine choked with dead bracken. Rust-colored water seeps from the hillside, pooling in the ruts left by some long-gone cart. Your hound ranges ahead, nose low, and returns with ears flat — nothing dangerous, just the cold smell of wet stone and rot.

You make ground. A jagged outcropping you've been watching as a landmark since morning is finally behind you now, and the distant silhouette of the crags is a little sharper against the grey sky. That's something.

But the going has taken a toll. The last of the smoked fish you packed is gone, eaten at midday because there was nothing else to hand. Your pack is lighter than you'd like.

**Journey to Deepcrag progress track: advanced (2 ticks marked, dangerous rank).**

You press on. The road doesn't get easier — but it does get shorter.
